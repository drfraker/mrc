Working with MRC

Efficient, pleasant, and genuinely supportive

Beyond providing the best support available, we work hard to make every engagement easy on your team — predictable rhythms, clear documentation, and no surprises on the invoice.

Our Approach

A steady weekly rhythm your team can count on

Every engagement is built around a consistent weekly review of your skilled Medicare patients, backed by consultants who are a phone call away the rest of the week.

During each review we work through every skilled patient with your team, assessing whether each one continues to meet Medicare Part A coverage guidelines — or whether a Notice of Non-Coverage is required. When a patient's coverage concludes, we complete a patient abstract and deliver it to your facility.

The Process

How an engagement works

Weekly patient review

We meet with your team about every skilled Medicare patient, verifying Part A coverage criteria and flagging when a Notice of Non-Coverage may be needed.

Clear documentation

Weekly patient abstracts are completed and delivered to your facility when each patient's Medicare coverage concludes — clean records, ready when you need them.

Always-on support

Your staff can call our consultants any time with Medicare questions — MDS scheduling, Consolidated Billing, payment classification, and more.

Peer review on demand

Our physicians review Inpatient, Emergency Department, and Office Visit charts, with the review process usually completed within two weeks.

Billing

Straightforward billing, no surprises

You'll always know what an engagement costs and what you're getting for it.

Monthly Retainer

Utilization review & support

A monthly retainer covers your weekly reviews and unlimited calls from your facility — every question your staff has, answered.

Per Patient

Discharge abstracts

End-of-month reports itemize a per-patient fee for each patient removed from Medicare coverage, so the invoice always matches the work.

Hourly

RAC appeal consulting

RAC consulting is billed hourly — you pay only for the time your appeal actually requires. Contact us for current rates.
