Resources

References we rely on

Tools and information we find useful — and often critical — in our work, shared in the hope your team finds them helpful too.

CMS Regulations & Guidance

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services' central library of regulations, manuals, and transmittals — the source of truth for Medicare policy.

cms.gov

Find-A-Code

A fast lookup for HCPCS, CPT, and ICD codes — handy for billing questions that come up during reviews.

findacode.com

SNF Consolidated Billing

CMS's reference for skilled nursing facility Consolidated Billing, including the current lists of exclusions.

cms.gov

AAPACN

The American Association of Post-Acute Care Nursing (formerly AANAC) — education and certification for nurse assessment coordinators.

aapacn.org

QIO Program

The CMS Quality Improvement Organization program — including the Beneficiary & Family Centered Care QIOs that handle appeals and quality-of-care reviews.

qioprogram.org
